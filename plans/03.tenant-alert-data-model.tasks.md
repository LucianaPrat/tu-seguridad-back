## Confirmed inputs

- Merge the existing schema; no production data must be preserved.
- Every camera has monitor zones; every zone has an alert level.
- Snapshot images stay in MySQL for now.
- Wire/database codes are English; frontend translates labels.
- A matching DVR `external_id` keeps camera configuration; absent discovered cameras become
  unconfigured.
- A user belongs to one space; a space has many users through `space_members`.
- Face Auth identities are multiple/revocable per user.
- Camera and zone deletion is logical.
- Providers receive a unique correlation id for inbound acknowledgement.

- Notification provider, retry policy and inbound-webhook authentication.
- Polling versus DVR WebSocket/push transport.

## Migration safety (T01)

- Confirmed by product owner: no production data must be retained.
- Development and test databases are treated as disposable for this migration.
- The migration may destructively replace `cameras`, `zones` and `zone_events`; the upgrade
  rehearsal in T08 is still mandatory before release.

## Migration constraint review (T02)

- `space_members.user_id` is unique and `(space_id,user_id)` is unique.
- `dvrs.space_id` is unique; `cameras(dvr_id,external_id)` is unique.
- `monitor_zones_rectangle_bounds_check` rejects negative coordinates, non-positive dimensions and
  rectangles extending beyond 100 percent.
- `alert_events` foreign keys use `SET NULL` for camera, zone and snapshot historical references;
  ownership and membership relations use `RESTRICT`.
- `snapshots.data` is `MEDIUMBLOB`; no schema field stores a DVR-derived snapshot URL.
