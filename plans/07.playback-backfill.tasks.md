## Decisions taken while building T01

The plan named the shape but left four things open. What was chosen, and what would reverse it.

| Decision                                                                                                                   | Reversed if                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reason` is a Prisma enum, not a free string, and it tags the `SNAPSHOT_KEEP_RAW` copy as `raw_copy` as well as the ledger | A third kind of audit row appears often enough that a migration per value is friction. Both values were named in the plan's own problem statement. |
| The metric is a `trigger` label on `pipeline_persons_detected_total`, not a new counter                                    | A dashboard needs ledger rows _written_ rather than ledger candidates _seen_ — the label counts the second, and the two differ only by the switch. |
| The trigger is threaded as a `PollTrigger` parameter through `pollGuarded`/`pollOnce`/`processImage`                       | A second thing needs to know the trigger, at which point it belongs on the captured image or on a request-scoped context instead of the signature. |
| The ledger's consumer is `scripts/export-ledger.ts`, not an HTTP route                                                     | Someone other than a developer at a terminal needs the frames. A route means auth, paging and a blob response for an audit tool with one user.     |

**Not done, deliberately:** the end-to-end walk-past check the plan's Verification section asks for
(turn the switch on, walk past a camera at night until the detector misses, confirm the row lands and
that `try-detect` reproduces the miss on the exported frame). It needs the recorder and a person
outdoors. Everything up to it is verified: 727 unit, 78 integration, 111 e2e.

## The three numbers that matter most

1. **One frame is a bad draw, and twenty is the whole win.** 2/10 events detected on the single frame
   the live path takes; 7/10 on twenty frames; 7/10 on forty. The decision rule asked for 2× and got
   3.5×.
2. **Night volume is tiny.** ~3.4 events/hour for the whole estate between 21:00 and 00:12. The plan's
   "~30 per camera per night" conjecture was about 5× too high.
3. **The median burst is one pulse**, not the four-to-five seen during a walk test.
4. **Our own threshold drops two thirds of what the detector finds** — 25 of 73 detections reach 0.45.
   Three of ten events had detections where every one fell below it.

## Open questions, in priority order

1. **Is `CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD` at 0.45 costing more than the sampling window ever will?** New, and it
   jumps the queue: two thirds of measured detections are discarded by it, and three of ten events
   produced nothing else. It is a one-line change with a false-positive cost nobody has measured,
   which is exactly the shape of thing that deserves a measurement rather than an opinion.
2. **Did channels 5 and 7 really see nothing for seven hours**, or is the recorder's classifier missing
   what they see? Three weeks of recording are available to check. If it is the latter, event-driven
   capture is blind on those cameras and no metric would show it. T00's method now applies directly:
   pull a window from either channel and score it.
3. **Is ffmpeg present on the production host?** Still open, but narrowed on 2026-09-10. It is
   unavoidable — the recorder has no playback-still endpoint (`playbackTime` is silently ignored and
   answers the _live_ frame with a `200`), and MediaMTX does not replace it, since its own documented
   way to extract stills is to run ffmpeg in a hook. What is new: `bluenviron/mediamtx` ships a
   `<version>-ffmpeg` tag and that service is already in `docker-compose.yml`, so this is a packaging
   question, not an architectural one. Also worth correcting: `decisions/002` rejected ffmpeg as a
   _live transcoder_ on cost-per-stream grounds, and says nothing about offline extraction.
4. **`ALERT_COOLDOWN_SECONDS`** — the owner set `.env.example` to `60` during this session and the edit
   was lost in a rebase. Schema default and README both still say `0`. If `60` is wanted, all three
   move together. There is a `stash@{0}` named `t07-alert-cooldown-wip` from that attempt.
5. **T05 has no metrics pipeline to alert on.** `ops/otel-collector/README.md` is explicit: the
   collector forwards **traces only**, and `GET /metrics` "is exposed for a scraper that does not exist
   here". So `dvr_event_motion_total` never reaches Grafana Cloud and no rule could evaluate. T05 needs
   a T05a in front of it — a `prometheus` receiver scraping the app and a metrics pipeline — and note
   the endpoint is gated by a custom `x-metrics-token` header, not bearer auth.

## How to resume

Everything operational is in [`docs/BEST_PRACTICES.md`](../docs/BEST_PRACTICES.md) under
"DVR motion events" and "Measuring against recorded video".

T01 is built and T00 is settled, so the plan is unblocked down to two owner decisions and one
unbuilt dependency. In rough order of value:

1. **Measure `CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`** (open question 1). Cheapest lever on the table and it needs no
   new machinery: the eleven TSVs in `docs/data/` already carry every `detScore`, so the recall side
   costs nothing but arithmetic. What is missing is the other half — how many false positives a lower
   threshold admits — and that needs frames with nobody in them, which the recorder has three weeks
   of.
2. **T02, with the window T00 measured.** `[T − 10 s, T + 0 s)` at 2 fps, 20 frames. Blocked only on
   deciding how ffmpeg reaches production (open question 3) — the `mediamtx:<version>-ffmpeg` tag is
   the shortest path, since that container already deploys.
3. **The ledger, end to end.** `SNAPSHOT_KEEP_MISSES=true`, walk past a camera at night until the
   detector misses, `npx ts-node scripts/export-ledger.ts /tmp/ledger`, and confirm `try-detect`
   reproduces the miss on the exported frame. The only part of T01 no test suite can stand in for.
4. **T05 needs T05a first** (open question 5) — there is no metrics pipeline for a rule to evaluate.

**Sampling recipe, for whoever runs the next T00-style measurement.** It is in
[`docs/BEST_PRACTICES.md`](../docs/BEST_PRACTICES.md) under "Measuring against recorded video", with
two traps worth repeating because both cost a run here:

- **`ffmpeg` reads stdin** and will silently eat the rest of a `while read` loop's event list. Pass
  `-nostdin`.
- **Do not convert time zones.** The recorder's clock has no offset and its search treats a `Z` suffix
  as local, so `date -u -d "$T - 15 seconds"` is wrong twice over — GNU `date` also parses the `- 15`
  as a UTC offset rather than a subtraction. Do the arithmetic on epoch seconds under `TZ=UTC`.
